Privacy Policy

GENMERCH — Suot Apparel · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GENMERCH — Suot Apparel (“GENMERCH,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit shopgenmerch.com, create an account, place and manage an order, or submit a Partner With Us application.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: your name and email address, and authentication details handled by our authentication provider. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive basic profile information Google provides, such as your name, email address, and profile image when available.
  • Order and delivery information: name, mobile number, delivery address and instructions, items ordered, order status, courier and delivery reference, and the receipt email address you provide.
  • Shopping information: cart contents, wishlist items, saved addresses or checkout drafts, and related preferences associated with your account or browser.
  • Payment preference and order records: the payment method you select and the payment/order status. Selecting GCash, Maya, or bank transfer records a preferred method only; the site does not automatically charge an online payment through this checkout.
  • Partner applications: if you use Partner With Us, we collect your name, optional business or brand name, email address, mobile number, city or province, partnership type, business stage, selling channels, products of interest, estimated order volume, optional shop or social-media link, preferred contact method, additional message, and your consent to be contacted about the application. We also create an application reference and use a keyed, one-way IP hash for basic rate limiting and abuse prevention; we do not store the raw IP address in the partner application record.
  • Technical information: basic browser or session information needed for the website, account security, abuse prevention, and keeping you signed in.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; operate the storefront; save carts and wishlists; receive and process orders; coordinate delivery; send order confirmation and shipping updates; respond to customer questions; prevent abuse; maintain transaction and inventory records; and improve the reliability of GENMERCH. If you submit a partner application, we use those details to review the inquiry, understand your intended partnership and order volume, prevent repeated or automated submissions, and contact you about the application using the method you select.

3. Google sign-in

If you use “Continue using Google account,” Google helps verify your identity. GENMERCH uses the basic identity information returned for sign-in and account identification. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or other Google services as part of this sign-in flow. We do not sell Google account data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate GENMERCH, including Supabase for authentication and database services and Resend for transactional emails. Partner application details are stored in a private Supabase table and are not publicly viewable. When you submit an application, the application details are emailed to the GENMERCH partnership contact through Resend; the email is configured so the team can reply to the email address you provided. We may share necessary order and delivery details with a courier or delivery service, and with a service provider when needed to fulfil a requested transaction or provide technical support. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect customers, the store, or the public.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Information is stored using our website and service providers. We apply access controls and other reasonable safeguards, but no internet service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage accounts, fulfil orders, review and follow up on partner applications, maintain business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use Google sign-in and may instead use another sign-in method offered by the store. You can clear locally saved shopping drafts using the controls provided on the site. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account or partner application information, contact us using the email below and include enough detail for us to locate the relevant record. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain order, application, or security records where needed for legitimate business or legal purposes.

7. Children and third-party services

GENMERCH is an online apparel store and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Links to third-party websites and services are governed by their own privacy practices; please review those practices when using them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GENMERCH changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.
