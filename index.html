About Us

GENMERCH — Suot Apparel is a Philippine online apparel and merchandise store built around everyday comfort, personal style, and practical essentials. Our collection includes wearable favourites such as T-shirts, shorts, sandos, and selected merchandise for everyday life.

We aim to make online shopping straightforward with clear product details, size and stock information, and delivery options shown during checkout. We also welcome entrepreneurs and creators who want to explore opportunities to grow with a local apparel brand.
