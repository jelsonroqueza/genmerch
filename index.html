About Us

GENMERCH — Suot Apparel is a Philippine-based online apparel and merchandise store built around everyday comfort, personal style, and practical essentials. Our collection features everyday favourites such as T-Shirts, Shorts, and Sandos, along with selected merchandise for different styles and routines.

Our goal is to make online shopping simple and clear through useful product details, available sizes, stock information, and order updates. Nationwide shipping and Cash on Delivery are available where shown at checkout. We want every customer to find apparel that feels comfortable, fits their style, and suits their day.
